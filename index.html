Booking & Rehearsal Pricing

Minimum of $60 just to open the door. You can stay up to 4 hours at that base rate.

Anything past the initial 4 hours is an additional $10 per hour.

Monthly Advance Discount: If you practice 1 day a week and pay in advance for the month, it is $200 total (which reduces it down to $50 per 4-hour session across 4 sessions).

Noise Ordinance Note: Full band setups with live acoustic drums or bass can book anytime on weekends, but are limited to after 3:00 PM on weekdays. Lower-volume acoustic sets or practice are fine anytime!
